Roy/SAC released his own ANSI and ASCII text artwork into the public
domain. His later clarification grants anyone the right to use the work
for any purpose without conditions where a public-domain dedication is
not legally possible. These imports are limited to Roy-authored files
from the Roy_ANSI.ZIP archive; they do not include the separately hosted
"other artists" gallery.
