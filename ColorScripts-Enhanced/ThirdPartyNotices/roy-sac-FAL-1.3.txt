Scope
-----

Every independently accessible file matching
ColorScripts-Enhanced/Scripts/roy-sac-*.ps1 is a modified Roy/SAC artwork
licensed under FAL-1.3. Each file header identifies the exact original ANSI
file, its source location or source archive, a SHA-256 checksum, the original
author, the applicable license, and the modification performed. The repository
root LICENSE expressly excludes these files from its Unlicense grant.

Modifications
-------------

ColorScripts-Enhanced contributors decoded the original CP437 ANSI streams,
rendered cursor and SGR operations through the repository's bounded terminal
emulator, and stored the resulting rows in safe PowerShell string literals.
Oversized works may be divided into separately accessible, numbered row
segments; those files identify their original row ranges. No authorship claim
is made over Roy's original artwork.

License chronology and conservative treatment
----------------------------------------------

Roy published a public-domain dedication in 2006 and his current gallery again
states that his ANSI and ASCII works are public domain. In 2008, however, he
announced a change to Free Art License 1.3 and requested copyleft treatment for
copies and derivatives. This repository follows the stricter 2008 statement
for every converted Roy artwork.
