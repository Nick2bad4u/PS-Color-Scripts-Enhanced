Project-specific scope
----------------------

The attested permission covers public redistribution in
ps-color-scripts-enhanced, conversion of ANSI or ICE streams into safe
PowerShell colorscripts, and logical vertical or horizontal splitting needed
to fit the gallery. Artist attribution remains required.

Each imported file header preserves an offline-readable title and artist
attribution plus a script-scoped link to the complete record. Group, pack,
canonical source URL, source checksum, license reference, rendered SHA-256
checksums, conversion details, and split coordinates are maintained outside
the published module at:

https://nick2bad4u.github.io/PS-Color-Scripts-Enhanced/docs/artwork.html

https://github.com/Nick2bad4u/PS-Color-Scripts-Enhanced/blob/main/audit/ArtworkProvenance.psd1
