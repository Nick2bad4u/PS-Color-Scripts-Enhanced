Permission attestation
----------------------

The scripts are used under fair use as this project is a non-commercial, educational, and archival gallery of 16colors artwork.

Project-specific scope
----------------------

The attested permission covers public redistribution in
ps-color-scripts-enhanced, conversion of ANSI or ICE streams into safe
PowerShell colorscripts, and logical vertical or horizontal splitting needed
to fit the gallery. Artist attribution remains required.

Each imported file header and ArtworkProvenance.psd1 entry identify the
original artist, group and pack when known, canonical source URL, source and
rendered SHA-256 checksums, conversion details, and source coordinates for
split works.

artwork. Its public archive availability is not treated as the source of this
permission. This notice records the maintainer's project-specific attestation;
